# Future Me — Condo Workspace

A future working life shown through an ordinary high-rise home office at night.

The current scene combines the workstation and imported plant, bookshelf and
articulated desk lamp with a room and a deep city view.

## Language

**Workstation**:
The desk, seated person and computer equipment forming the main subject. The pair's desk and person belong to this part of the scene.

**Room setting**:
The surrounding home-office interior: walls, floor, window, lighting fixtures, plant and text-free wall art.

**City view**:
The layered towers, visible building corners, rooftops and illuminated windows
outside the condo window, viewed from above street level.
